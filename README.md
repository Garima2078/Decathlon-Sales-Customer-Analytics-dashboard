# Decathlon-Sales-Customer-Analytics-dashboard
This project features an interactive Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard designed for Decathlon. The primary objective is to monitor overarching business performance, evaluate product and sports category profitability, track customer acquisition and retention trends, and uncover seasonal purchasing behaviors to optimize retail strategies.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/Garima2078/Decathlon-Sales-Customer-Analytics-dashboard/blob/main/Raw%20Data.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Key Business Questions
As a analyst evaluating this dashboard, several key business questions are addressed:
Revenue & Growth: What are the total sales and order volumes, and how do they track against growth targets?   
Product Performance: Which sports types and product categories drive the highest revenue and order counts?   
Seasonality: How do sales fluctuate across different months of the year, and when do peak buying periods occur?  
Customer Demographics: Is there a significant variance in purchasing behavior between male and female customers?   
Customer Retention: What is the overall customer retention rate, and how stable is customer loyalty month-over-month? 

## Important KPIs
The dashboard highlights several core performance indicators-
Total Sales: 367M (reflecting a 40.8% positive trend)  
Total Orders: 30K (reflecting a 40.1% positive trend)   
Total Customers: 8,998 (reflecting a 13.5% growth)  
Average Order Value (AOV): ₹12,236 (reflecting a 0.48% change)  
Overall Customer Retention Rate: 64.58%

## Insights
Total Sales: 367M, showing strong upward momentum with a growth rate of 40.8%.   
Total Orders: 30K, reflecting a significant 40.1% increase.   
Performance vs. Targets: Both metrics show substantial ~40% double-digit expansion, indicating strong demand and robust top-line business growth.  

Top Revenue Generator (Sports Type): Cycling leads by a wide margin, generating 81M in sales across 3,139 orders.   
Top Product Category: Cycling accounts for the largest share of overall product category revenue at 22%.   
Other Major Drivers:
Outdoor: 41M sales (3,020 orders)   
Running: 35M sales (3,151 orders)   
Gym: 33M sales (3,116 orders)   
Hiking: 33M sales (2,848 orders)   
Secondary Categories: Football (11%), Badminton (9%), Fitness (9%), Camping (9%), Yoga (7%), Trekking (7%), Swimming (7%), Cricket (2%).  

Peak Period: Sales reach their highest levels in spring, peaking in April at 35M and March at 33M.   
Mid-Year Dip: Demand experiences a notable slowdown during the late summer months, dropping to 27M in August and September and 28M in July and November.   
Steady Baseline: Sales remain steady around 31M–32M during the winter months (January–February) before climbing into spring.   

Sales Distribution: Revenue is almost evenly split between genders:   
Male: 50.1% (184M in sales)   
Female: 49.9% (183M in sales)   
Variance: There is no significant gap in overall revenue contribution between male and female customers, showing balanced market engagement across genders.  

Overall Customer Retention Rate: 64.58%.   
Monthly retention fluctuates throughout the year within a range of ~12.8% to 17.3%.   
Retention is highest in April (17.29%), January (16.60%), and March (16.21%).  
Retention hits its lowest point in December (12.81%) and August (13.31%).   

## Final Conclusion
Decathlon's analytics reflect a thriving retail operation characterized by robust double-digit growth in sales (+40.8%) and orders (+40.1%). Because Cycling serves as the primary financial engine, supply chain and inventory efforts should prioritize cycling gear, especially ahead of peak spring demand. Furthermore, the balanced gender split suggests marketing campaigns are successfully reaching a broad audience. Strategic initiatives should focus on boosting engagement during the summer sales dip and improving loyalty programs to counter the drop in year-end monthly retention.
